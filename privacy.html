Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 4, 2026

1. Introduction

At Comunalco, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our web application.

2. Data We Collect

  • Identity Data: Email address and name provided via Google OAuth login.
  • Application Data: Utility metrics, tariffs, property names, and tenant information that you manually enter.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and usage statistics collected via basic analytics tools.

3. Payment Information

We do not store credit card or bank details. All transactions are securely handled by Lemon Squeezy. They act as the Merchant of Record and have their own privacy policy regarding your financial data.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase cloud infrastructure. We implement industry-standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or modification of your data.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • The right to access the data we hold about you.
  • The right to rectify inaccurate data.
  • The right to request the deletion of your data ("Right to be forgotten").
  • The right to data portability.

6. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your login session and store your theme preferences (Dark/Light mode).

7. Contact

For data requests or privacy questions, please email us at support@comunalco.com.

1. Вступ

В Comunalco ми поважаємо вашу приватність і прагнемо захищати ваші персональні дані. Ця політика пояснює, як ми збираємо та використовуємо вашу інформацію.

Ви маєте право на перегляд, зміну або повне видалення своїх даних з нашої системи. Для цього ви можете звернутися до нашої служби підтримки.
