Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 4, 2026

1. Introduction

This Policy describes how Comunalco ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your data. We are committed to ensuring your privacy is protected in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data Collection

  • Account Data: We collect your email and name via Google Login (OAuth 2.0).
  • Usage Data: We store utility metrics, property names, and history that you enter into the application.
  • Cookies: We use session cookies to keep you logged in and store theme settings.

3. Payment Security

All payments are processed by PayPro Global. We do not store or process your credit card numbers or banking information on our servers. PayPro Global's privacy policy governs their use of your financial data.

4. Data Storage and Third Parties

Your data is stored on encrypted cloud servers provided by Supabase (PostgreSQL). We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, or delete your personal data. You can request account deletion at any time by contacting our support.
